The Cottage

A single-storey stone barn, converted in 2019, with oak beams, underfloor heating and a wood burner for cosy evenings.

  • Sleeps 4
  • 1 double, 1 twin
  • 1 bathroom with walk-in shower
  • Dog friendly (1 dog)
  • Step-free access

Inside

Living room: sofas, a wood burner (logs provided), a smart TV and a shelf of books and games.

Kitchen: oven, hob, dishwasher, fridge-freezer, microwave and a coffee machine. Dining table for four.

Bedrooms: one king-size double and one twin. Bed linen and towels are included.

Bathroom: walk-in shower, heated towel rail and toiletries.

Outside

A private, fenced garden with a patio, table and chairs, and a barbecue. Parking for two cars beside the cottage.

Good to know

  • Check-in from 4pm, check-out by 10am
  • Minimum stay 3 nights
  • One well-behaved dog welcome (£20 per stay)
  • No smoking
  • Welcome hamper on arrival
  • Travel cot and high chair on request
  • We live on site and are happy to help
